# Slide 2: Introduction
**Title:** What is Avatarsi?
* **Concept:** A Java Spring Boot web application designed to enhance the learning and teaching experience using AI.
* **Goal:** To bridge the gap between scattered study materials and structured learning.
* **Core Idea:** Acts as a smart companion that understands documents, plans study schedules, and recommends resources.

---

# Slide 3: The Problem Statement
**Title:** Challenges in Modern Education
* **Information Overload:** Students and teachers deal with massive PDFs and documents that take hours to read.
* **Poor Time Management:** Students struggle to plan their study schedules effectively before exams.
* **Resource Discovery:** Finding high-quality, relevant video tutorials (like YouTube) is time-consuming.
* **Information Retrieval:** Searching for specific topics inside multiple downloaded PDFs is difficult.

---

# Slide 4: Key Features of Avatarsi
**Title:** Core Capabilities
1. **Automated Note Summarization:** Upload PDFs and instantly get AI-generated summaries.
2. **Dynamic Study Plan Generation:** Input a topic and exam date to get a day-by-day roadmap.
3. **Smart Document Search:** Search for specific concepts across all your uploaded materials.
4. **Curated Video Recommendations:** Automatically suggests relevant YouTube tutorials based on your current topic.

---

# Slide 5: Feature Spotlight - Document Summarization
**Title:** From Long PDFs to Quick Notes
* **How it works:** Uses Apache PDFBox to read uploaded documents and an AI Service to extract key takeaways.
* **Benefit:** Saves hours of reading time by converting heavy textbook chapters into concise, readable short notes.
* *Visual Idea: Show a screenshot of the web interface where a PDF is uploaded and the summary text appears.*

---

# Slide 6: Feature Spotlight - Intelligent Study Planner
**Title:** Structured Learning Roadmaps
* **How it works:** Calculates the days remaining until an exam and divides the time into 3 strategic phases.
* **Phase 1 (Basics):** Foundational reading and video watching.
* **Phase 2 (Practice):** Problem solving and mock tests.
* **Phase 3 (Revision):** Short notes and weak-area focus.
* **Benefit:** Prevents last-minute cramming and promotes consistent learning.

---

# Slide 7: Value for Teaching (Why it is Worth It)
**Title:** Empowering Educators and Teachers
* **Curriculum Planning:** Teachers can use the Study Planner to divide their syllabus based on the final exam date.
* **Instant Handouts:** Educators can upload their lengthy lecture notes and instantly generate summaries to give to students as quick-reference handouts.
* **Flipped Classroom:** Teachers can quickly find and attach relevant YouTube links generated by Avatarsi for pre-class assignments.
* **Student Support:** The document search feature allows teachers to quickly locate exactly where a concept was mentioned in their course material when answering student doubts.

---

# Slide 8: Technical Architecture
**Title:** Under the Hood (Tech Stack)
* **Backend Framework:** Java Spring Boot (Robust, scalable, and secure).
* **Document Processing:** Apache PDFBox (Industry standard for PDF manipulation).
* **AI & Logic Layer:** Custom `AIService` handling chronological math (Java Time API) and text processing.
* **Frontend:** HTML/CSS (Clean, user-friendly interface).
* **Design Pattern:** MVC (Model-View-Controller) ensuring clean code separation.
